Which tables? For how long? Who signs off?

Context lives in Slack threads and meeting notes, not in the ticket.

How it works

From a one-line request to a decision you can trust

  1. Identify

    Reads the ticket and flags what's missing: system, scope, duration, approver.

  2. Find

    Pulls context from meeting notes, your policy, past tickets and the requester's project.

  3. Autofill

    Writes what it found back into the ticket in Jira, ServiceNow or your desk.

  4. Send

    Asks the requester or system owner in Slack or Teams for anything still unclear.

  5. Suggest

    Recommends approve, narrow or deny, and shows the policy and precedent behind it.
